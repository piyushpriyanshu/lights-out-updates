# Lights Out updates

Latest version: **8.3** (24 Oct 2026)

The Lights Out app checks this page and offers new versions by itself.
To install by hand, download [LightsOut-8.3.apk](LightsOut-8.3.apk).

## What's new
- A new look: one premium typeface, liquid-glass cards and calmer text across the app
- Team colours: pick your team in Settings and the whole app takes its colour
- Live: replay player with lead changes, retirements, fastest lap and flags on the timeline
- Live: commentary card under the map, Timing (sectors and pace), Pit lane times, weather row and a race-control timeline
- Settings rebuilt: My paddock (your driver gets a star), time format, true black and liquid glass switches
- Nothing shown twice: this weekend's race, tiles and commentary each appear once
