# Lights Out updates

Latest version: **9.0** (25 Oct 2026)

The Lights Out app checks this page and offers new versions by itself.
To install by hand, download [LightsOut-9.0.apk](LightsOut-9.0.apk).

## What's new
- Live tab during a race weekend: a finished session shows its results (top three, classification, timing, race control, radio) with Replay and Share recap; a session to come shows a countdown
- Share recap makes a share card picture, dark or light, to save to your photos or share
- Timing: lap times that included time in the garage (17:23.067) are gone, and the final order follows the official classification
- Team radio: clips are written out by themselves, and a new Best speech model with F1 words makes the text far closer to what is said
