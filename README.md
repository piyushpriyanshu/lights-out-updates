# Lights Out updates

Latest version: **7.1** (14 Oct 2026)

The Lights Out app checks this page and offers new versions by itself.
To install by hand, download [LightsOut-7.1.apk](LightsOut-7.1.apk).

## What's new
- Engine sound when the app opens: the car flies past left to right (switch in Settings; quiet when the phone is on silent)
- Full-screen map: the track on top, the order with tyres and the picked driver's speed, throttle, brake, revs, gear and sectors below
- Races > Tech: every part teams have declared to the FIA in 2026, by team and Grand Prix, with the part lit up on the car
