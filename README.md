# Lights Out updates

Latest version: **9.4** (01 Nov 2026)

The Lights Out app checks this page and offers new versions by itself.
To install by hand, download [LightsOut-9.4.apk](LightsOut-9.4.apk).

## What's new
- Stewards live: every penalty and investigation as it happens — a Stewards list, +5/DT/SG/INV marks in the order, a banner, and on the lock-screen Live Update
- Results switch to F1's official classification once it's out (penalties after the flag included); before that, unserved time penalties are added and marked provisional
- Share card: the winner shows the race time, like the others' gaps
- Fixes: Races tab froze during races; pit stops on the driver's own lap; stale results after a replay; half-loaded stats now retry; and more
- Reminders on time on Android 13+, in your current time zone, and they follow a moved session
- Offline note, Settings > Problem report, recovery if the page crashes
