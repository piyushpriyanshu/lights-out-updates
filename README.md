# Lights Out updates

Latest version: **9.3** (25 Oct 2026)

The Lights Out app checks this page and offers new versions by itself.
To install by hand, download [LightsOut-9.3.apk](LightsOut-9.3.apk).

## What's new
- Race grid: F1's official starting grid with grid penalties applied, as soon as it is published
- Results straight after a race or Sprint from F1's own timing (and the podium in the calendar), until the official results arrive
- Always current: results and standings refresh during race weekends, when you come back to the app, and in the background
- Track maps: Straight Mode zones and Overtake points from the FIA circuit map; cars in a zone glow green
- Driver and team stats counted as formula1.com does: poles, Grands Prix entered, DNFs, DSQ and DNS
- Corrected qualifying times and pit stop counts in replays and weekend results
- Rulebook and course fixes (Q3 is 13 minutes, 768 kg minimum weight and more); US GP tech updates
